# Bitcoin Business Models

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Building a profitable company purely focused on Bitcoin is difficult due to its disintermediating nature. Viable business models generally fall into six "Hard" categories, rejecting the "Easy" but harmful paths.

## The Challenge

Bitcoin is a protocol designed to disintermediate third parties. Therefore, attempting to build a classic middleman business model is structurally difficult.

### "Easy" (Harmful/Non-Viable) Models

These models go against the ethos or legal realities of the ecosystem:

1.  **Selling Customer Data:** Violating user privacy for profit.
2.  **Printing Shitcoins:** Minting free assets (ICOs, tokens) to fund a project without providing fundamental value, often relying on regulatory arbitrage.
3.  **Illicit Activity:** Ransomware or outright fraud.

### "Hard" (Genuine) Models

Giacomo Zucco identifies six main patterns for genuine entrepreneurship built _around_ the Bitcoin ecosystem:

1.  **Financial Services Toll:** Exchanges and brokers (High profit, huge regulatory/security risk).
2.  **Software / Consulting:** Coding and advisory.
3.  **Operational Functional Roles:** Providing infrastructure (Mining pools, Lightning nodes/LSPs).
4.  **Hardware Development:** Uncopyable physical goods (Wallets, Nodes, ASICs).
5.  **Purely Monetary Use:** Leveraging Bitcoin to optimize an unrelated business (Corporate treasury, evading international fees).
6.  **Knowledge/Education Broker:** Selling intelligence and workshops to traditional industries trying to understand the transition.
