# Proof of Work (Unforgeable Costliness)

[[index|← Return to Index]]

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Proof of Work (PoW) is the mechanism that binds digital information to physical reality through energy expenditure, making history immutable.

## What Is It?

To prevent rewriting history, Bitcoin requires "Unforgeable Costliness." Proof of Work (PoW) is the digital equivalent of stamping a document with expensive, unforgeable ink.

Mining is the process of spending energy to find a hash that meets the network's difficulty target. This energy expenditure cannot be faked or "virtualized."

## Why Does It Matter?

PoW replaces the trusted third party (like a newspaper publisher or a central bank). Instead of trusting an entity to tell the truth, we trust the laws of thermodynamics.

It enables the "Timechain" to maintain a single, agreed-upon history. To change the past, an attacker would need to expend more energy than the entire honest network combined, which becomes economically and physically prohibitive.

## The Mechanism

- **The Hash:** Miners run SHA-256 millions of times per second.
- **The Target:** They must find a result below a specific number.
- **The Difficulty Adjustment:** Bitcoin's "internal thermostat" adjusts every 2,016 blocks (~2 weeks) to ensure blocks are found on average every 10 minutes.
