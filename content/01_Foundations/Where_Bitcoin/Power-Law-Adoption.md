# Power Law Adoption

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Bitcoin's growth follows a power law relationship, suggesting its adoption behaves like a biological system or a network effect rather than a simple financial bubble.

## What Is It?

A power law is a functional relationship between two quantities where a relative change in one results in a proportional relative change in the other ($\text{price} \propto \text{time}^x$).

Bitcoin's long-term price and adoption metrics (like address count or hashrate) have historically tracked power law curves when plotted on log-log scales.

## Why Does It Matter?

- **Predictability:** While short-term prices are volatile, the long-term trend follows a mathematically predictable corridor.
- **Natural Phenomenon:** Power laws are found in city sizes, galaxy distributions, and internet topology. This suggests Bitcoin is a "natural" discovery of the digital age.
- **Counter-Bubble Argument:** Simple bubbles collapse and never return to their previous trend. Bitcoin has survived multiple 80%+ crashes while staying within its power law corridor.
