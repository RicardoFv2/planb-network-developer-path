# Regulatory Arbitrage (Stablecoins)

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Stablecoins (like USDT) allow users to bypass local capital controls, proving that the demand for borderless, censorship-resistant value transfer exists even for fiat assets.

## What Is It?

Regulatory arbitrage is the practice of taking advantage of discrepancies between different regulatory environments.

Stablecoins allow users in countries with strict capital controls or failing currencies (like Argentina or Turkey) to access U.S. dollars digitally without going through a local bank.

## Why Does It Matter?

- **Proof of Concept:** Stablecoins prove that people value the "rails" of crypto (24/7, borderless, peer-to-peer) even if they aren't ready for Bitcoin's volatility yet.
- **The BitTorrent of Finance:** Just as BitTorrent proved that media piracy was a service problem, stablecoins prove that financial restriction is a service problem.
- **Stepping Stone:** Stablecoins act as a gateway drug for Bitcoin adoption, onboarding users to digital wallets and private keys.
