# The Double-Spending Problem

## The Core Challenge
In the physical world, money (like a gold coin) cannot be in two places at once. If I give you a coin, I no longer have it. In the digital world, data is just a collection of bits that can be copied infinitely for zero cost.

**Double-Spending** is the act of spending the same digital token twice.

## Pre-Bitcoin Solutions
Before 2008, every digital cash system relied on a **Centralized Clearinghouse** (a Mint or a Bank).
1. User A sends a digital token to User B.
2. User B checks with the Central Mint: *"Has this token been spent before?"*
3. The Mint checks its ledger. If it's the first time, the Mint marks it as "spent" and authorizes the transaction.

## The Flaw of Centralization
Relying on a central mint creates:
- **Censorship Risk:** The mint can refuse to process transactions for certain people.
- **Single Point of Failure:** If the mint goes offline or is seized by a government, the entire system dies.
- **Privacy Loss:** The mint sees every transaction.

## Bitcoin's Innovation
Bitcoin solved the double-spending problem **without a central authority** by using a distributed ledger (Blockchain) and Proof of Work.
