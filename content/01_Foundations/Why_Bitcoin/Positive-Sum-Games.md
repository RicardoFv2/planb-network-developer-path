# Positive-Sum Games

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Trade is a positive-sum game where both parties benefit, creating wealth out of voluntary cooperation.

## Types of Games

- **Positive-Sum (Wealth Creation):** "I want your bread, you want my eggs." We trade, and we both end up better off than before. Total wealth in the system increases.
- **Zero-Sum (Wealth Transfer):** A game like Poker where for one person to win, another must lose. Total wealth remains the same.
- **Negative-Sum (Destruction):** Theft or violence. "I punch you and take your bread." Wealth is transferred, but also destroyed (your face hurts, resources are spent on weapons instead of production).

## The Role of Money

Money is a tool that allows positive-sum games to scale. It solves the **Double Coincidence of Wants**, allowing a bread-maker to trade with an egg-maker even if they don't need each other's goods at the exact same moment.

## Why It Matters

A sound monetary system encourages society to focus on positive-sum games (production/trade) rather than negative-sum games (theft/debasement), leading to a more peaceful and prosperous civilization.
