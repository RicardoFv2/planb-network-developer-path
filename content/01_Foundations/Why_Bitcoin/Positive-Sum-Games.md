# Positive-Sum Games

**Definition:**
A scenario where the total gains and losses of all participants are greater than zero. Voluntary trade is positive-sum because both parties believe they are better off after the trade.

**Why it matters:**
Civilization is built on positive-sum games�on free trade and cooperation. Bitcoin is the ultimate tool for scaling these games globally. By providing a neutral, trustless layer for value exchange, Bitcoin allows people who don't trust each other to engage in mutually beneficial trade without the risk of being robbed by a centralized 'Big Pirate.' It is the economic engine of a peaceful, decentralized society.
