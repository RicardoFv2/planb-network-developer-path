# Money as a System of Control

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Digital fiat money is a centralized information system that can be used to monitor, control, and exclude individuals from the economy.

## The Problem

Digital money today (Cards, Apps, Bank Transfers) is not actual money; it is **Centralized Information**. This gives intermediaries total power over your ability to transact.

## Methods of Control

1.  **Surveillance:** Every transaction is tracked, tagged, and analyzed by banks and governments.
2.  **Financial Exclusion:** AML/KYC laws act as a "Pre-Crime" system. If the system's filters flag you, or if you belong to a "disliked" group, your access to your own wealth can be deleted.
3.  **Social Engineering:** Governments can use programmable digital currencies (CBDCs) to force specific spending behaviors.

## The Cypherpunk Solution

Bitcoin returns us to a **Bearer Instrument** model. Transactions are peer-to-peer and do not require permission from a central server. This decouples your wealth from the system's control.
