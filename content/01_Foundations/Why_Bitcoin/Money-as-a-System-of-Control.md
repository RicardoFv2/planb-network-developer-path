# Money as a System of Control

**Definition:**
The use of financial infrastructure as a tool for surveillance, social engineering, and political enforcement.

**Why it matters:**
As money 'virtualized' into digital ledgers, it became the ultimate leash. Governments can use 'financial de-platforming' as a pre-emptive punishment for dissidents. Bitcoin is the antidote because it returns money to being a neutral tool. By separating money and state, Bitcoin prevents the financial system from being used as a weapon, ensuring that my right to participate in the economy is based on math, not political permission.
