# Stock-to-Flow Ratio

**Definition:**
An analytical measure of scarcity. It is the relationship between the total existing supply of a good (Stock) and the amount of new units produced in a year (Flow). A higher S2F ratio means it takes more years to replicate the current supply, making the asset more resistant to debasement.

**Why it matters:**
This is vital for understanding why Bitcoin is 'Hard Money.' Most commodities have a low S2F; if price goes up, we produce more, crashing the price. Gold was money because its flow is tiny (~2%) relative to its stock. Bitcoin is the only asset with **perfectly inelastic supply**. No matter how high the price goes, the protocol's math ensures the flow stays low and predictable. For a developer or business owner, saving in a high S2F asset like Bitcoin means hard work won't be diluted by a 'printer.'
