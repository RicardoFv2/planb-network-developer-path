# Fractional Reserve Banking

**Definition:**
A banking system where only a small fraction of bank deposits are backed by actual cash/gold on hand.

**The Analogy:**
Giacomo uses the **Parking Lot Analogy**: A lot owner who rents out your car to someone else while you aren't looking. If everyone asks for their car back at the same time, the system collapses (Bank Run).

**Why it matters:**
It allows for artificial credit expansion and creates systemic risk. It is a form of 'virtualizing' value where multiple people think they own the same physical asset.
