# Roman Currency Debasement

**Definition:**
The practice of mixing precious metal coins with cheaper base metals (copper) to increase the money supply.

**Why it matters:**
Giacomo uses the Roman *Denarius* as a warning. By debasing the currency to fund wars and welfare, the Empire destroyed the savings of its citizens, leading to hyperinflation, cultural decay, and eventually, the collapse of Roman civilization itself.
