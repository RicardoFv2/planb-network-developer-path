# Inelasticity of Supply

**Definition:**
Inelasticity refers to a situation where the supply of a good does not increase significantly even if the demand (and price) goes up.

**Why it matters:**
Bitcoin is the only asset in the universe with **perfectly inelastic supply**. Even if the price goes to $10 million per coin, the issuance remains capped at 21 million units. This is the ultimate defense against debasement.
