# The History of Coinage

**Definition:**
The transition from raw commodities (gold/silver) to standardized, stamped weights known as coins.

**Why it matters:**
Coinage (originating in places like the Temple of Juno Moneta) solved the problem of *portability* and *divisibility*. However, it introduced a new risk: **Trust**. Users had to trust the issuer that the purity and weight of the coin were as stated. This trust opened the door for future debasement.
