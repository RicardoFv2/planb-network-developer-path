# Bearer Instrument vs Credit

**Definition:**
A **Bearer Instrument** is a form of value where the holder (the bearer) is the legal owner (e.g., physical cash or Bitcoin). **Credit** is a debt obligation where I don't own the money, but a 'promise' from a bank to pay me back.

**Why it matters:**
This is the cornerstone of financial sovereignty. Modern digital money is mostly 'Credit' on third-party ledgers (banks). This makes us subject to permission: if a bank doesn't like your business, they can freeze your account. Bitcoin is the world's first **Digital Bearer Instrument**. When I hold my private keys, I own the asset directly with no counterparty. This shifts the balance of power from centralized institutions back to the individual.
