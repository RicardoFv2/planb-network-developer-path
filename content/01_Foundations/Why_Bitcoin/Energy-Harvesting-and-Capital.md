# Energy Harvesting and Capital

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Real wealth is stored energy. Capital is the result of harvesting energy today to increase productivity tomorrow.

## The Concept

In the physical world, work requires energy. When Crusoe catches fish, he is "harvesting" energy from the environment. When he saves that fish, he is storing that energy for later.

## Capital Accumulation

**Capital** is anything that increases the efficiency of energy harvesting.

- A **fishing rod** is capital because it allows Crusoe to harvest more fish (energy) with less effort.
- A **net** is even higher-order capital.

## Why This Matters for Bitcoin

Bitcoin's Proof of Work (PoW) is a mechanism for anchoring digital value in the real world's energy expenditure. By requiring energy to create Bitcoin, the network ensures that the digital ledger reflects a "work" that cannot be forged. It turns electricity into a digital form of stored energy (Capital).
