# The Skinny Pirate Analogy

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Cryptography is a shield that allows individuals to protect their wealth even against those with superior physical force.

## The Analogy

- **The Big Pirate:** Represents the state or any powerful entity with superior physical strength (violence).
- **The Skinny Pirate:** Represents the individual (you).
- **The Treasure:** Digital wealth or information.

Normally, the Big Pirate can just beat up the Skinny Pirate and take his gold. But if the Skinny Pirate uses **Cryptography**, he can hide his treasure map in his head or encrypt it with a key.

Because the map is encoded, the Big Pirate's physical strength becomes useless. He can punch the Skinny Pirate, but he cannot punch the password out of his brain or force the mathematics to unlock.

## Why It Matters

For the first time in history, cryptography gives the "Skinny Pirate" a defensive advantage. It turns the balance of power back towards the individual, making the cost of seizing wealth much higher than the benefit for the "Big Pirate."
