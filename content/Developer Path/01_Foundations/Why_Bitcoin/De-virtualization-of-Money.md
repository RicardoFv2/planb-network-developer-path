# De-virtualization of Money

**Definition:**
The process of returning money to a bearer instrument state within a digital environment. Bitcoin is 'Digital Gold' that has the sovereign properties of a physical object.

**Why it matters:**
Historically, virtualization (gold -> paper -> credit) increased convenience but destroyed sovereignty. Bitcoin is the first successful attempt at **De-virtualization**. It keeps digital speed but restores the 'permissionless' nature of a physical shell or coin. It allows us to move 'digital objects' that nobody can print or delete, combining the best of the physical and digital worlds.
