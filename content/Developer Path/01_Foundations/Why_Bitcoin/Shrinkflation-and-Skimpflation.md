# Shrinkflation and Skimpflation

**Definition:**
**Shrinkflation** is when product size decreases but price stays the same. **Skimpflation** is when quality or service degrades while the price remains stable.

**Why it matters:**
These expose how currency debasement rots the economy. Even if official inflation numbers look stable, quality of life collapses because everything becomes smaller or worse. It creates a 'Fiat Culture' of disposable goods. For a business owner, it's a warning: inflation forces you to degrade your product to survive. Bitcoin fixes the money, which in turn fixes the incentives, allowing businesses to focus on long-term quality and excellence.
