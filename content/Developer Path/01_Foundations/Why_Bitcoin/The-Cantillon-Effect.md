# The Cantillon Effect

**Definition:**
Describes the uneven distribution of new money. The first recipients of newly created money (banks, governments) benefit at the expense of those who receive the money last, after prices have already risen.

**Why it matters:**
This reveals why the fiat system is a wealth redistribution machine. Those at the 'center' buy assets at 'old' prices using new money. By the time that money reaches the average worker, inflation has already kicked in. Bitcoin fixes this by having a transparent, decentralized issuance. No one is 'closer' to the printer; the rules apply to all nodes equally, removing the structural advantage of the political and financial elite.
