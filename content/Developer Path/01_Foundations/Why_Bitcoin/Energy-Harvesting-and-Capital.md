# Energy Harvesting and Capital

**Definition:**
The process of human progress through the accumulation of energy-efficient tools.

**Why it matters:**
Progress is the movement from human muscle to animal, steam, oil, and nuclear energy. This is only possible through **Capital Accumulation** (saving). Bitcoin, as a store of value, is a way to preserve this 'economic energy' across time and space without it being stolen by inflation.
